# Shoe Mold (Sole Mold) Manufacturing: Industry Benchmarks and Technology Trends (Vietnam / Taiwan / China / Korea), 2024–2026

Research date: 2026-10-19. Method: about 25 web searches and fetches. **Caveat on sources:** several primary pages (shoemakersacademy.com, globalprecision.vn, oureman.com, chinatimes.com) could not be fetched directly, because DNS failed or the proxy returned 403. Findings from those pages rest on search-engine extracts of their text. Mark them "snippet-based" when citing. Public, quantified, footwear-specific benchmarks are **scarce**. Most shoe-mold makers are private SMEs that publish little. Several findings below come from general mold making and are labelled that way.

---

## 1. Leading shoe mold makers and the technology they publicize

### Takeaway
No public source ranks the "leading" shoe-mold makers. The ecosystem is anchored by Taiwanese footwear OEM groups (Pou Chen/Yue Yuen, Freetrend, Hong Fu, Chinli) with plants in Vietnam, Indonesia and China. Mold makers serving them are mostly private SMEs, some China-based with sister plants near HCMC. The machine shops that publish anything mostly advertise high-speed 3-axis and 4-axis CNC. Five-axis and robot cells show up only in a few Chinese examples (for example a 12-CNC + 1-robot shoe-mold line).

### Cited Findings
- **Pou Chen Group** (Taiwan) runs production in China, Indonesia, Mexico and Vietnam. Its subsidiary Yue Yuen is described as the world's largest athletic-shoe manufacturer. — [Wikipedia: Pou Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pou_Chen_Corporation); [Pou Chen locations](https://www.pouchen.com/index.php/en/about/locations)
- Taiwan-based footwear makers **Freetrend, Oasis Shoe Industry and Hong Fu** run Vietnam and Indonesia factories on a system "exported from Taiwan". **Chinli Plastic Industrial** (founded 1990 in Taiwan) makes plastic and rubber components in Vietnam and Indonesia. — [CommonWealth Magazine, 2023-09](https://english.cw.com.tw/article/article.action?id=3503); [SourceReady list](https://www.sourceready.com/supplier/list/shoe-sole-manufacturers)
- A Taiwanese shoe firm based in Vietnam reportedly develops about **1,000 new Adidas models per year** (title-level only; the article body could not be fetched). — [China Times, 2024-04-27](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20240427000014-260410)
- **Yuan Shen Metal Mold Co.** (Chang'an, Dongguan, South China) is a full-service outsole mold design and fabrication shop. It has a **sister factory outside HCMC**: customers develop in China, then have production molds made in Vietnam. It reportedly runs **6 high-speed CNCs**. (Older info: the post dates from about 2019, snippet-based.) — [Shoemakers Academy factory tour](https://shoemakersacademy.com/footwear-outsole-mold-production-tour-a-mold-factory/)
- **Global Precision (Binh Duong area, HCMC)** makes EVA, Phylon, PU and rubber sole molds and offers repair and refurbishment. Its own page cites "advanced high-speed **3-axis and 4-axis** CNC milling", with no 5-axis claimed (snippet-based). — [Global Precision](https://globalprecision.vn/en/cnc-shoe-mold-processing-with-high-precision-to-create-a-prestigious-brand/)
- **Chinese example of shoe-mold line automation:** EMAN (益模) designed a flexible automated machining line for **Zhansheng Mold (站胜模具)**, a shoe-mold maker. The line has **12 CNC machines + 1 robot arm** and covers program upload, workpiece kitting, robot transfer, **on-machine probing and centering (在机分中)** and CNC machining. Expected results: **6 fewer people per machining shift team** and **+20% equipment efficiency** (vendor case, snippet-based, date not confirmed). — [EMAN/oureman case](https://www.oureman.com/view_618.html)
- Zhejiang Dongdubao Mould (EVA, TPR, PU and rubber shoe molds) claims **30,000 pairs of molds per year** (directory listing, unverified). — [SourceReady](https://www.sourceready.com/supplier/list/shoe-sole-manufacturers)
- Taiwanese machine builder **YCM (永進)** lists shoe-mold machining as a target application for its 5-axis, vertical, horizontal and gantry machines. — [YCM](https://www.ycmcnc.com/classification/1533013833403)
- Chinese machine vendors push dedicated **5-axis shoe-mold mills**, for example the KAIBO DC6060A. They claim cycle time is cut by "nearly 30%" through single-clamping. This is vendor marketing with no named customer. — [KAIBO DC6060A](https://www.kbcncmachinery.com/news/5-axis-shoe-sole-mold-milling-machine-dc6060a-a-cost-effective-solution-for-mold-manufacturing.html); [KAIBO 5-axis article (zh)](https://www.kaibomachines.com/zh/news/innovative-shoe-last-manufacturing-case-study-of-five-axis-cnc-milling-machines.html)
- **Korea (Busan):** a survey of **52 Busan mold companies** found **38.2% want 5-axis machining centers** but cite lack of funds and staff. The planned Busan "Makers Valley" mold cooperation center would install **14 equipment types, including 5-axis HSM and CMM**, plus **shared 5-axis CAM licences**, to cut development time and lead time. These are general mold firms, not shoe-specific. — [Busan root-industry Makers Valley report, 2025 (CLIK/NANET PDF)](https://clik.nanet.go.kr/clikr-collection/policyinfo/2/8/2025/CLIKC5272561714199700_attach_1.pdf)

### Inferences
- A Korean-owned HCMC shop with about 30 MCTs and PowerMill is likely **at or above the publicly visible norm** for Vietnamese shoe-mold makers on spindle count. Local competitors advertise mainly 3- and 4-axis HSM. The differentiators being publicized in China are cell automation (robot + probing + MES) and 5-axis single-setup machining, not spindle count.
- The Zhansheng line gives a concrete, comparable reference: about 1 robot per 12 machines, an automated kitting and probing workflow, and +20% OEE. A 30-MCT shop could treat this as a "2–3 cells" scenario.

### Gaps
- No public list or market share of the top shoe-mold makers by country. No public technology statements from the main Taiwanese mold suppliers to Pou Chen, Feng Tay or Chang Shin, nor from Korean-owned mold shops in Vietnam.
- No verified Korean shoe-mold company (Busan or in Vietnam) publicizing 5-axis or automation cells was found.
- Jinjiang (晋江): more than 60% of above-scale firms use "数控一代" (CNC-generation) equipment, but this is not mold-specific ([Jinjiang gov, 2024-04](https://www.jinjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/jjyw/202404/t20240422_3029912.htm)). No Jinjiang shoe-mold technology data was found.

## 2. Lead times and size-run machining approach

### Takeaway
The only public shoe-mold lead-time benchmark is old (about 2019). It puts a size run of wood or master cuts at **3 days** and a **full metal size-run set at about 40 days** for a 6-CNC shop. Korean R&D in 2017 put the conventional mold-to-product cycle at about **40 days**. Size grading is done with 3D CAD grading tools such as Procam or proprietary systems, which generate per-size geometry from a base size. No public source describes "one CAM template per size run" automation in PowerMill.

### Cited Findings
- Yuan Shen (6 high-speed CNCs): **size run of wood cuts in 3 days; full set of metal molds in 40 days**. Indicative prices: **about USD 1,400 per rubber outsole mold** (CNC-cut steel, Teflon-coated) and **USD 1,800–2,000 per cast-aluminum EVA midsole pressing mold**. (About 2019, snippet-based.) — [Shoemakers Academy](https://shoemakersacademy.com/footwear-outsole-mold-production-tour-a-mold-factory/)
- Process routes: outsole mold metal "may be cast, burned by EDM, or CNC cut". **Flat rubber outsole molds are typically steel.** **Midsole molds with high sidewalls and undercuts are cast aluminum.** — [Shoemakers Academy: How outsole tooling is made](https://shoemakersacademy.com/shoe-outsole-tooling-made/)
- Conventional sole-mold chain = **design → wood pattern (목형) → sand mold (주물사) → casting (4 steps)**. Metal 3D printing can skip straight from design file to part. — [KISTI report TRKO201900002030](https://scienceon.kisti.re.kr/srch/selectPORSrchReport.do?cn=TRKO201900002030)
- Adidas/Carbon reporting: metal sole molds "take **four to six weeks to cast and grind**" and need about **10,000 uses** to pay back (2017). — [Singularity Hub, 2017](https://singularityhub.com/2017/04/07/adidas-to-mass-produce-3d-printed-soles-in-vats-of-warm-liquid-goo/)
- **Grading technology:**
  - The CN1260670C patent covers full-size automatic grading of 3D shoe-mold models from triangle meshes (scan-capable) via a grading table plus a geometric-relationship table. — [CN1260670C](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN1260670C/en)
  - CN1316709A covers setting CAM parameters on the base size, after which the software derives the other sizes. — [CN1316709A](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN1316709A/en)
  - US 6473667 describes per-size dimensions computed and fed to NC machining. — [USPTO 6473667](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/6473667)
  - WO2003088778A2 sends per-size data to NC or CAM. — [WO2003088778A2](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003088778A2/en)
- Outsole-mold CAD/CAM tools named in industry overviews: **Procam** (footwear CAD/CAM producing CNC-ready mold files), Autodesk Fusion/PowerShape, and Yuan Shen's in-house tooling. Pattern grading tools: Shoemaster, Icad, MiriSys, Ruizhou. The "near-100% grading accuracy" claims are vendor-style. — [The Footwear Edge](https://thefootwearedge.com/digital-transformation-in-footwear-top-global-software/)
- Last grading must follow size intervals of the EU, UK, US and Mondopoint systems, and CAD/CAM automation of grading is an active research topic. — [Int J Adv Manuf Technol (Springer)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00170-015-7947-8)
- A 2026 MDPI paper proposes a **data-driven parametric framework for size-adaptive shoe-insole outline generation**, which shows parametric sizing is current research. — [MDPI Applied Sciences 16(18):9042](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/18/9042)
- Footwear injection scheduling literature cites a **mold change of about 30 minutes per station**. This gives context for how many molds per size the production side needs. — [IEOM 2021](https://www.ieomsociety.org/singapore2021/papers/889.pdf)

### Inferences
- A full size run (often about 10–15 sizes × L/R) is effectively a **family of near-identical parts**. That favors (a) graded CAD from a base size and (b) CAM templates or macros where toolpaths are regenerated per size with the same strategy and tool list. In PowerMill this maps to templates, macros and automation scripts. No public footwear case documents this, so it is a likely internal differentiator.
- The 40-day full-set benchmark is old. Brand pressure (section 3) suggests leading shops now aim for much shorter turnarounds on development (sample) molds, but no current public number was found.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 published lead-time figures for sample vs. production size-run molds in Vietnam, Taiwan, China or Korea.
- No public PowerMill, hyperMILL or Tebis documentation of a shoe-mold "size-run template" workflow or its time savings.
- No data on typical machining hours per mold or per size run.

## 3. Brand-driven trends (Nike, Adidas, etc.) and impact on Vietnam mold makers

### Takeaway
Brands have cut development time mainly through 3D-printed prototypes and printed midsoles (Nike Express Lane; Adidas/Carbon 4D). This mostly squeezes the **sample/development mold** stage and raises the bar on speed. Steel and aluminum production molds remain the volume baseline. Korea's FIT pursued 3D-printed provisional molds to cut a roughly 40-day cycle to under a week (2017; no later outcome found).

### Cited Findings
- **Adidas Futurecraft 4D (Carbon DLS):** midsole print time cut from 90 to 20 minutes. The product launched **11 months** after the partnership vs. the typical **15–18 months** from design to store. (2017–2018.) — [Carbon case study](https://www.carbon3d.com/resources/case-study/adidas); [Harvard RCTOM](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-rctom/submission/the-perfect-fit-adidas-sprints-ahead-with-3d-printing/)
- **Nike Express Lane (from 2016)** cut supply-chain timelines from months to weeks. Nike said 3D prototyping was **16× faster** than prior methods (2018). — [Thomasnet](https://www.thomasnet.com/insights/nike-adidas-3d-printing/); [Harvard RCTOM](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-rctom/submission/adding-value-in-nikes-production-line/)
- **Korea (FIT, 한국신발피혁연구원):** under its "신발지능형 공장 구축사업" (smart shoe-factory project), it aimed to cut the **about 40-day mold-to-product cycle to within 1 week**. The method is direct 3D printing of provisional molds (가금형) by photo-curing, non-metal, with a patent filed (2017). — [ETNews, 2017-08-10](https://www.etnews.com/20170810000373)
- KISTI report: metal 3D printing of shoe-sole molds was done with a domestic company and skips the 4-step casting chain. — [KISTI](https://scienceon.kisti.re.kr/srch/selectPORSrchReport.do?cn=TRKO201900002030)
- Vendor claim: 3D-printed shoe molds cut delivery time by **20%** and raise durability by 20%. SLA resin master patterns replace CNC wood patterns for sand casting (single vendor, unverified). — [ChanHon Tech](https://chanhontech.com/3d-print-solutions/3d-printing-of-shoes/)
- Patent literature covers **sole structure masters/molds with textures and indicia** formed in the master or mold, which is relevant to digital texturing. — [USPTO 9474327](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/9474327)
- **Sustainability/process example:** a safety-footwear maker (6 lines, >1M pairs/yr) switched to a water-based mold release and reported **−20% release agent, +33% midsole-outsole bond, −75% scrap**. — [Chem-Trend case](https://chemtrend.com/case-study/reduced-waste-increased-performance-in-footwear-manufacturing/)
- **Vietnam's position:** Vietnam makes **more than 50% of global branded-footwear OEM output** (Taiwan-business view, UDN). Footwear exports were forecast at about **USD 27 billion** in 2024–25. The industry association (Lefaso) has proposed industrial zones for tanning, technical fabric and **mold components**. — [UDN](https://udn.com/news/story/6842/8339293); [Vietnam News](https://vietnamnews.vn/economy/1689902/footwear-industry-set-to-gain-27-billion-in-export-this-year.html)
- **Tariffs:** Busan shoe firms in Vietnam were hit by a US "46%" reciprocal tariff announcement (April 2025). — [Busan Ilbo, 2025-04-03](https://www.busan.com/view/busan/view.php?code=2025040318263366764)

### Inferences
- For a Vietnam mold shop, the brand trend means more SKUs, more frequent design changes and shorter sample windows. Fast-turn development molds (including 3D-printed or hybrid inserts) and quick revision of graded sizes become competitive levers, not only machining cost per mold.
- Tariff uncertainty pushes footwear OEMs to source tooling locally in Vietnam, which favors in-country mold makers over imports from China.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 brand-published data on mold lead-time requirements for Vietnam suppliers.
- No confirmation that Nike or Adidas currently specify 3D-printed metal production molds.
- No found source on laser texturing adoption rates in Vietnamese shoe-mold shops.

## 4. CAM case studies (PowerMill / hyperMILL / Tebis / Cimatron / WorkNC) in shoe or tire molds

### Takeaway
No quantified, footwear-specific CAM vendor case study (PowerMill or competitors) was found in 2024–2026 public sources. Autodesk's footwear stories are brand or design oriented (Under Armour), and Delcam's dedicated footwear software was discontinued under Autodesk. Only generic mold-CAM claims are available.

### Cited Findings
- Autodesk's footwear story is **Under Armour**, a Fusion 360 generative-design midsole and lattice project. It is design-focused, not a mold shop. — [Autodesk: Under Armour](https://www.autodesk.com/customer-stories/under-armour)
- **Delcam's footwear-specific software** (formerly Delcam Crispin) was not continued or supported by Autodesk. — [Wikipedia: Delcam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delcam)
- Autodesk markets "shoe design and manufacturing software" (Fusion, PowerShape, PowerMill), but with no quantified mold-shop result on the page. — [Autodesk shoe design](https://www.autodesk.com/solutions/design-manufacturing/consumer-products/shoe-design-software)
- Torrenza Mould Craft adopted PowerMill, PowerShape and Fusion (a general mold shop with no footwear numbers). — [Autodesk partner story](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/success-stories/torrenza-mould-craft-achieving-their-goals-with-autodesk-solutions/8174)
- **hyperMILL:** OPEN MIND reported a new 5-axis cavity strategy cutting programming from **5 hours to 5 minutes** in one case, combinable with hyperMAXX roughing (older, 2011, general mold). — [CTE Magazine](https://ctemag.com/products/hypermill-3/)
- WorkNC and Tebis are described in practitioner forums as the high-end mold CAM systems, alongside one other, in German automotive tooling. This is anecdotal. — [Practical Machinist](https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/mold-making-worknc-vs-tebis-differences.431955/)

### Inferences
- The lack of public footwear CAM case studies suggests that CAM automation in shoe molds (size-run templates, macro-driven programming) is mostly in-house know-how. A factory that documents its own PowerMill template gains (for example programming hours per size run) would itself become a rare benchmark.

### Gaps
- No PowerMill, hyperMILL, Tebis, Cimatron or WorkNC footwear-mold or tire-mold case study with quantified results was found. Tire-mold cases were not specifically retrieved.

## 5. Automation in high-mix, low-volume mold shops

### Takeaway
Pallet systems (Erowa / System 3R) combined with cell controllers and robots let one-off mold shops reach a reported **85–90% spindle utilization** and run unattended overnight. The Chinese 12-CNC + robot shoe-mold line shows the same model applied to footwear molds. Key enablers are standardized zero-point pallets, on-machine probing, and kitting and presetting off-line.

### Cited Findings
- **PrecisionX Group:** an Erowa cell controller runs **two 5-axis MCs** lightly attended or unattended around the clock. Spindle utilization is **up to 90%**, and rush jobs or **one-piece prototypes** are inserted mid-run without disrupting the schedule. — [MMS: Cell Controller Boosts Production Flexibility](https://www.mmsonline.com/articles/cell-controller-boosts-production-flexibility)
- **Industrial Molds (Rockford, IL):** automated cells since 2002, with RFID-tagged pallets and electrode holders. Robotic cells run **lights-out**. Output is now higher with **2 wire EDMs and 1 operator**, vs. 6 operators on 11 machines before. — [PlasticsToday](https://www.plasticstoday.com/automation/industrial-molds-completes-automated-moldmaking-cell)
- **Matrix (mold shop):** palletizing moved blocks between machines without re-fixturing, which raised spindle utilization. One robot served two EDMs. — [MoldMaking Technology: Standardized Tooling and Automation](https://www.moldmakingtechnology.com/articles/standardized-tooling-and-automation-take-the-lead-role)
- **Makino (vendor):** automation in die and mold can push utilization toward **about 85%** in high-mix, low-volume settings. Build-A-Mold's robot graphite cell plus pallet changer gave shorter cycle times and consistent quality. — [Makino: Build-A-Mold](https://www.makino.com/en-us/resources/content-library/customer-testimonials/build-a-mold-transforms-operations); [Erowa CNC automation](https://www.erowa.com/en/solutions/cnc-automation)
- **Unverified vendor figures:** spindle utilization rose from **52% to 89%** and from **61% to 93%** (unnamed shops). — [AMD Machines](https://amdmachines.com/solutions/machine-tending/)
- **Shoe-mold-specific:** Zhansheng's 12 CNC + 1 robot line, with on-machine centering, expects **−6 staff per team and +20% efficiency**. — [EMAN case](https://www.oureman.com/view_618.html)
- **Low-cost route:** SelfLube used a **Universal Robots cobot + Haas MC** for high-mix small batches. — [MoldMaking Technology](https://moldmakingtechnology.com/articles/an-impossible-automation-project-still-proves-invaluable-)
- **Vietnam labor driver:** Taiwanese firms in Vietnam face labor shortages and respond with raises, campus recruiting **and automation** (robot arms adopted, CNA, 2025-11). — [CNA, 2025-11-25](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/aopl/202511250020.aspx)

### Inferences
- Shoe-sole mold halves (cavity plates, cores) are often similar in size within a size run, and sometimes across models. That makes **standard zero-point pallets plus robot loading** more feasible than in general one-off mold shops. The main barriers are fixture standardization across mold sizes and reliable probing for each part.
- For a 30-MCT factory, a phased route would be: (1) standardize zero-point pallets on all machines (off-line setup); (2) presetting plus tool-life management; (3) 1–2 robot or pallet-pool cells on the most uniform part families (size-run cavity plates); (4) night-shift unattended running with in-process probing. This mirrors the Zhansheng and PrecisionX models.

### Gaps
- No Korean or Japanese shoe-mold shop automation case was found. No public data on unattended-hour ratios in Vietnamese mold shops.

## 6. Materials, cutting tools, speeds, spindle speeds

### Takeaway
Rubber outsole molds are typically CNC-cut steel. EVA, Phylon and PU midsole molds are mostly aluminum (cast, or machined 6061-T6 / 7075). Chinese shoe-mold machines advertise **24,000 rpm** spindles as standard. Generic aluminum mold guides cite **12,000–24,000 rpm (6061)** and **10,000–20,000 rpm (7075)**. No shoe-specific cutting-data benchmark was found.

### Cited Findings
- Steel is used for flat rubber outsole molds, and cast aluminum for high-sidewall, undercut midsole molds. — [Shoemakers Academy](https://shoemakersacademy.com/shoe-outsole-tooling-made/)
- Rubber outsole molds: CNC-cut steel with **Teflon coating**. EVA midsole: cast aluminum (about 2019). — [Shoemakers Academy tour](https://shoemakersacademy.com/footwear-outsole-mold-production-tour-a-mold-factory/)
- Aluminum shoe-mold suppliers cite **7075 and 6061-T6**, CNC and die-casting, and a claimed mold life above 1M shots (vendor claims). — [FJ Shoes Mould (Made-in-China)](https://fjshoesmould.en.made-in-china.com/product/jOYGrZlVLuWv/China-Aluminium-Shoes-Mould-Shoe-Outsole-Making-Mold-Made-in-CNC-Process.html)
- Shoe-mold CNC listings advertise **≤24,000 rpm, 3.0 kW water-cooled spindles** for aluminum and steel engraving. — [Remax CNC listing](https://remaxcnc.en.made-in-china.com/product/HwuGjSsCkftX/China-CNC-Machine-for-Mold-Machine-Shoe-Sole-Mould-Making-Machines.html); [BuyCNC](https://buycnc.com/cnc-mill-aluminum-cnc-machine-for-mold-making/)
- Generic 5-axis aluminum mold guide: **6061: 12,000–24,000 rpm; 7075: 10,000–20,000 rpm**. — [Omni CNC guide (ko)](https://www.omni-cnc.com/aluminum-mold-making-step-by-step-guide-to-using-a-5-axis-cnc-machine/)
- An older Korean patent (KR19990007720A) describes outsole cavities formed from a cured silicone + aluminum powder mix, an alternative non-machined route. — [KR19990007720A](https://patents.google.com/patent/KR19990007720A/ko)

### Gaps
- No published shoe-mold-specific feeds, speeds, tool brands or step-over standards. No data on rpm distributions in leading Taiwanese or Korean shoe-mold shops (for example whether 30–40k rpm spindles are used for fine texture or logo work).
- No source on laser texturing vs. ball-mill texturing adoption in shoe molds.

## 7. Vietnam-specific context

### Takeaway
Vietnam's mold sector is growing (more than USD 1B per year, about 18% growth, secondary source), but localization is low, and high-end tooling and materials still come largely from China. Labor is tightening, with mid-skill shortages and emigration. Power reliability is a known risk, with a 2023 blackout cost of about USD 1.4B and a projected deficit of about 4,200 MW from 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Vietnam's mold and precision engineering sector is **more than USD 1B per year, growing about 18%/yr** (MOIT, via a secondary blog). Domestic makers supply only **about 8.5% of injection molds** and sit at tech levels 3–4 of 5; levels 1–2 depend on imports. — [AMSVN blog](https://amsvn.com/en/blog/vietnams-mold-market.php)
- Domestic firms meet only **about 10%** of supporting-industry demand for materials and components. China was the top source of textile, garment, leather and footwear supporting products (Jan–May 2025). — [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnams-strategic-response-to-us-tariffs-supporting-industries-in-focus.html/)
- About **55%** of Vietnamese footwear enterprises cite dependence on imported components as a major factor (2024 paper). — [IJAAS 2024](https://www.science-gate.com/IJAAS/Articles/2024/2024-11-08/1021833ijaas202408009.pdf)
- A trade database lists **39 Vietnamese footwear-mold exporters** (shipment-level, not national stats). — [Volza](https://www.volza.com/p/footwear-mould/manufacturers/manufacturers-in-vietnam/)
- **Trade fair:** at the **2026 Shoes & Leather Vietnam** Korean pavilion, Busan TP reported **89 export consultations worth USD 3.11M**, and shoe-machine maker USMC signed an order with a Vietnamese maker. — [Nate News, 2026-08-26](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260826n32451)
- **Labor:** more than **100,000 young people per year** leave Vietnam for higher wages abroad, causing factory shortages (2026-08). Mid-skill workers are expected to decline over the next 5–10 years while high-skill scarcity grows. — [Nate News, 2026-08-09](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260809n12599); [전국인력신문](https://www.kjob.news/news/529950)
- **Power:** the 2023 outages cost about **USD 1.4B (0.3% of GDP)** (World Bank). A shortfall of **about 4,200 MW and 2.9 billion kWh** is projected for the next year. — [InCham Hanoi](https://inchamhanoi.vn/en/power-outages-cost-vietnam-1-4b-world-bank); [Briefs.co](https://www.briefs.co/news/vietnam-warns-power-shortages-could-start-next-year/); [SupplyChainBrain](https://www.supplychainbrain.com/articles/37454-vietnams-factories-boost-operations-as-power-shortages-ease)
- Vietnam's footwear supply chain is sensitive to Chinese port disruption. Port efficiency, **power reliability** and industrial-zone capacity are flagged bottlenecks (2025 industry analysis by a Chinese shoe firm, so a potentially biased source). — [Heva Shoes, 2025](https://hevashoeinc.com/china-vs-vietnam-footwear-manufacturing-in-2025/)

### Inferences
- **Power risk + lights-out machining:** unattended night shifts in Vietnam need UPS or ride-through for controllers and spindles, restart-safe programs (PowerMill toolpath splitting, restart points) and possibly on-site generation or solar. The 2023 outages hit the north hardest, but a 2027 deficit is national.
- **Climate:** a hot, humid HCMC climate means thermal drift and humidity affect fine finishing on aluminum molds. This argues for climate-controlled finishing bays and warm-up cycles. This is a general machining inference; no Vietnam-specific shoe-mold source was found.
