# Consulting and Engineering-Support Services for a Korean-Owned Shoe-Mold Factory near HCMC (PowerMill / ~30 MCTs)

Research date: 2026-10-19. Method: web search (about 19 searches). Two direct page fetches failed (autodesk.com and kitech.re.kr DNS errors), so most findings come from search-result summaries of official pages, not from full reads of each page. Treat contact details and partner tiers as needing confirmation. No pricing was found for any provider. Every cost figure below is either cited or marked as unknown.

## 1. Autodesk PowerMill resellers/partners offering consulting (Vietnam and Korea)

### Takeaway
In HCMC, **OneCAD Vietnam** is the best-documented PowerMill partner. Autodesk-published case studies show it delivering on-site PowerMill installation and one-on-one training to Vietnamese machining firms. In Korea, **YC Corporation (와이씨코퍼레이션)** and **Delcam Korea / HD Solutions (한국델켐)** both offer PowerMill training, and YC also advertises post-processor building (포스트 프로세서 구축). For deeper macro/API automation, Autodesk partners outside Asia (OptiNC, E&C Solutions, Arkance) advertise remote PowerMill automation and post work.

### Cited Findings
**Vietnam (HCMC)**
- OneCAD Vietnam is listed in Autodesk Partner Finder. It is described as an Autodesk-only reseller and service provider with certified technical staff, and its address is on Nguyen Hue, District 1, HCMC — [Autodesk Partner Finder: OneCAD Vietnam](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/onecad-vietnam/1385); [Geospatial World profile](https://resource.geospatialworld.net/company/onecad-vietnam)
- Autodesk partner success stories show OneCAD supplying PowerMill with on-site installation and one-on-one training (customer: Kenstone) and dedicated PowerMill training for A Chau Engineering's engineers — [Overcome the COVID pandemic with Autodesk PowerMill](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/success-stories/overcome-the-covid-pandemic-with-autodesk-powermill/9315); [Tackle complex surfaces with PowerMill advanced toolpaths](https://www.autodesk.com/asean/support/partners/success-stories/tackle-complex-surfaces-with-autodesk-powermill-advanced-toolpaths/7800)
- VietCAD Co., Ltd describes itself as an Autodesk Golden Partner covering CAD/CAM, with consulting and training — [Autodesk Partner Finder: VietCAD](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/vietcad-co-ltd/1334)
- Tan Duc Technical Development & Trading JSC (TD&T) reports more than 29 years in software distribution and consulting and gold-reseller status across Autodesk verticals. Its profile does not specifically mention PowerMill — [Autodesk Partner Finder: TD&T](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/td-t-tan-d-c/1390)
- Several of these firms call themselves the "top" or "only" authorized partner, and these claims conflict. Tier and status were not independently verified (search-summary caveat).

**Korea (remote, or visiting Vietnam)**
- YC Corporation (와이씨코퍼레이션, ycgroup.co.kr) presents itself as an official Autodesk partner in Korea. It runs online and offline training centers and offers technical services including design outsourcing and **post-processor building (포스트 프로세서 구축)**. Its PowerMill page covers high-speed 3/4/5-axis machining — [YC Corporation PowerMill page](https://www.ycgroup.co.kr/powermill); [YC CAD/CAM business](https://www.ycgroup.co.kr/main_cadcam)
- Delcam Korea (한국델켐, delcam.co.kr; the page shows HD Solutions) runs PowerMill training at its Seoul HQ education center and mentions manufacturing-process consulting — [delcam.co.kr CAD/CAM 전문 교육](https://www.delcam.co.kr/estore_education)
- A 2016 article reports free PowerMill/PowerShape/ArtCAM/FeatureCAM training and a PowerMill skills certificate exam run through this channel. **This is old and may no longer be current** — [전자신문 2016-08-19](https://m.etnews.com/20160819000376)
- Autodesk Korea's manufacturing "standardization" solutions page (process standardization and templates) is a possible starting point for Autodesk-side consulting — [Autodesk KR standardization](https://www.autodesk.com/kr/solutions/manufacturing-software/standardization)

**Global remote PowerMill automation and post specialists**
- OptiNC (Brazil, Autodesk partner) sells the "Plugin OptiNC for PowerMill". It also offers standardized CAM automation implementation and custom CNC post-processor development and optimization — [Autodesk Partner Finder: OptiNC](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/optinc/937); [OptiNC Consultoria](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/optinc-consultoria-ltda/2780)
- E&C Solutions (Autodesk partner) offers bespoke post-processor development, programming consultancy, process development, optimization and automation — [Autodesk Partner Finder: E&C Solutions](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/e--c-solutions/1893)
- Arkance Systems delivered a PowerMill project that included macros to automate common tasks and post-processor tuning (Murtfeldt/Auron case) — [Autodesk success story](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/success-stories/innovation-und-przision-durch-die-partnerschaftliche-zusammenarbeit-zwischen-murtfeldt-und-auron/7932)
- Trisita configured post-processors for a customer's mills after a site visit and trial — [Autodesk success story: effective training and support](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/success-stories/effective-training-and-support/9871)
- PowerMill exposes macro scripts, a Python API and .NET/COM interfaces for automation. This comes from a third-party API directory, so check it against Autodesk docs — [API Evangelist: Autodesk PowerMill](https://providers.apievangelist.com/providers/autodesk-powermill/)
- Autodesk Partner Finder lists PowerMill post-processor starter-pack training offerings — [Autodesk PowerMill buy page / partner offers](https://www.autodesk.com/products/powermill/buy)

### Inferences
- Practical route: use OneCAD (HCMC) for on-site Vietnamese-language training and license/support, and YC Corporation or Delcam Korea for Korean-language template/post-processor work (remote, or a short visit). A specialist automation partner (OptiNC-type) is optional, for macro/plugin-based standardization of shoe-mold toolpath templates.
- None of the Vietnamese partners publicly advertise "process optimization / template standardization" as a packaged service. The factory should ask for it explicitly, scoped as: PowerMill template library, tool database, post-processor verification for each machine control, and a cycle-time benchmark.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for any partner (training day rates, post-processor fees, consulting).
- Whether any HCMC partner has Korean-speaking engineers is unknown.
- The Autodesk Consulting (direct) offering in ASEAN/Korea for PowerMill could not be confirmed. The autodesk.com fetch failed.
- Other Korean PowerMill training firms (independent instructors, 폴리텍 courses) were not surfaced by search.

## 2. Cutting-tool makers' application engineering in Vietnam

### Takeaway
Search found little direct evidence of tool-maker technical centers inside Vietnam. Most brand presence in HCMC runs through distributors. Mitsubishi Materials states that its SEA Machining Technology Center provides test cutting, training, technical consulting and production-line diagnoses. Sandvik Coromant runs 20+ Productivity Centres worldwide, but search did not confirm one in Vietnam. YG-1 has a small 100%-owned Vietnam subsidiary.

### Cited Findings
- Mitsubishi Materials (SEA) says it established a Machining Technology Center to solve customers' cutting problems through test cutting, training courses and technical consulting, including chip-control and tool-life tests and production-line diagnoses. Vietnam appears only as a country selector on the site, with no address shown — [MMC SEA: application technology](https://www.mmc-carbide.com/sea/solution/applications/tech)
- Sandvik Coromant runs "over 20" Productivity Centres worldwide. Older sources give 26 (2012), and no Vietnam centre was found. Its application specialist roles include analyzing customers' technical needs and proposing solutions, which is pre-sales engineering. It also runs a "Made for Milling" training course, at Halesowen UK — [ScandAsia: Productivity Center opening](https://scandasia.com/opening-of-sandvik-coromant-productivity-center/); [Sandvik careers posting](https://www.home.sandvik/en/careers/job-search/engineering/R0063770/); [Sandvik Made for Milling course](https://www.sandvik.coromant.com/gb/events_training/pages/milling-course.aspx)
- A third-party directory lists Sandvik Coromant among cutting-tool suppliers in HCMC (unverified) — [ensun.io cutting tool Vietnam](https://ensun.io/search/cutting-tool/vietnam)
- YG-1 Vietnam is described as a 100% directly invested subsidiary of YG-1 (Korea), with 10–24 employees. No HCMC technical-support function is described — [VietnamWorks: YG-1 Co., Ltd](https://vietnamworks.com/nha-tuyen-dung/yg-1-co-ltd-c157018)
- A Vietnamese supplier advertises itself as authorized distributor for OSG, YG-1, ZCC.CT and Sandvik Coromant. Its page is an event listing — [Vietnam Industrial Fiesta](https://vietnamindustrialfiesta.com/authorized-distributor-osg-yg-1-zcc-ct-sandvik-coromant/)
- MTA Vietnam (HCMC, 1–4 July 2026) is the main precision-engineering show, with industry seminars. It is a practical venue to meet tool-maker application engineers. No specific milling seminars by MMC, OSG or Sandvik were listed — [MTA Vietnam](https://www.mtavietnam.com/)

### Inferences
- The usual industry model, not verified for Vietnam in this research: tool makers and their distributors give free tool trials and machining audits when there is a realistic chance of tool-spend conversion. A 30-MCT mold shop is large enough to get this. The factory should ask distributors for a free "productivity audit" or trial on a representative shoe-mold job (e.g., roughing an Al/steel sole mold, then fine ball-end finishing).
- For shoe molds, the most relevant small-diameter ball and high-feed endmill brands are Korean and Japanese: YG-1, Korloy, OSG, MOLDINO (formerly Hitachi Tool), NS Tool, Union Tool and MMC. Korean brands (YG-1, Korloy) are the most likely to offer Korean-speaking support.

### Gaps
- Not found or verified: Korloy Vietnam presence; OSG Vietnam (direct subsidiary vs distributor); MOLDINO, NS Tool, Union Tool, Kennametal or Iscar Vietnam offices; any paid audit programs or pricing.
- Whether Sandvik Coromant has a Vietnam legal entity or productivity centre was not confirmed.

## 3. Machine-tool builders' technology centers in Vietnam

### Takeaway
**Makino** has the strongest local application capability: a 4,700 m² Technology Centre in Saigon Hi-Tech Park (opened 2019) with a showroom, training and application engineering. Makino is strong in high-speed mold machining. **DN Solutions** (Korean, formerly Doosan Machine Tools) set up DN Solutions Vina in HCMC in 2024 for sales and technical support. **DMG MORI** has an HCMC head office. **Heidenhain** is represented by AMS Co., Ltd (HCMC), which is also its service support centre.

### Cited Findings
- Makino Vietnam Technology Centre opened in 2019 in Saigon Hi-Tech Park (4,700 m², two storeys). It houses a showroom, training facility, parts centre and offices, and offers sales, local service, training, technology support and application engineering. The details date from 2019 — [VIR](https://vir.com.vn/makino-opens-vietnam-technology-centre-to-strengthen-customer-care-69697.html); [Makino press release](https://makino.com.cn/newsroom/press-releases/makino-opens-vietnam-technology-centre)
- DN Solutions announced its Vietnam subsidiary DN Solutions Vina in HCMC in March 2024 to strengthen sales and technical support across Southeast Asia. Address: M.O.R.E Building 2F, 40A-40B Ut Tich St., Ward 4, Tan Binh, HCMC — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/dn-solutions-global-machine-tool-company-enhances-product-sales-and-technical-support-across-southeast-asia-with-new-subsidiary-in-vietnam-302076768.html); [DN Solutions locations](https://www.dn-solutions.com/us/contents/company-location.do)
- DMG MORI Vietnam Co., Ltd (HCM head office): 235-241 Cong Hoa St., Tan Binh, HCMC. A separate Hanoi office exists. No technology centre was confirmed — [DMG MORI operation bases](https://www.dmgmori.co.jp/corporate/en/company/base.html)
- Okuma Vietnam Co., Ltd has a presence in HCMC (Facebook page). No technology centre was confirmed — [Okuma Vietnam Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Okumavietnam/)
- Heidenhain's global contact list names AMS Co., Ltd as its Vietnam distributor: 243/9/10D To Hien Thanh St., Ward 13, District 10, HCMC, +84 28 3868 3738, info@amsvn.com. AMS describes itself as the licensed distributor and service support centre for Heidenhain across Vietnam — [Heidenhain around the world](https://www.heidenhain.com/about-us/contact/heidenhain-around-the-world); [AMS Heidenhain distributor](https://amsvn.com/en/authorized-distributor-of-heidenhain-in-vietnam.php)
- Haitian Precision (Hision CNC) has a Binh Duong factory and distributes mold-oriented CNC machines — [Hision CNC Vietnam](https://hisioncnc.vn/en/)

### Inferences
- The OEM of the factory's existing MCTs is the first call for control parameter tuning: high-speed or look-ahead settings, AI contour control (Fanuc), and Heidenhain cycle 32 tolerance. That tuning interacts directly with PowerMill output (tolerance, point distribution, arc fitting). DN Solutions is the obvious Korean-language option if the fleet includes Doosan or DN machines.
- Makino's centre is the best local venue to benchmark high-speed finishing strategies on a shoe-mold test part, even if the factory does not buy Makino machines. This is usually tied to a sales relationship.

### Gaps
- Not found or verified: Hyundai WIA, Hwacheon, Fanuc, or Siemens Vietnam application centers and their services; whether any builder sells paid process-improvement consulting; pricing.

## 4. Metrology / accuracy services (Renishaw, Hexagon, Zeiss, API)

### Takeaway
No Vietnam-based Renishaw ballbar or laser calibration service was confirmed. Renishaw's own accredited calibration lab serves only US customers, and it trains in the UK and US. The factory should ask Renishaw's regional office or local distributors, or machine OEM service teams (who often own ballbars), for ballbar and laser checks.

### Cited Findings
- Renishaw's ISO 17025 (A2LA) calibration service facility is in the US and "currently only available to USA customers". Recommended recalibration intervals: QC20 ballbar 1 year, XL-80 laser 3 years. Customers are told to contact their local Renishaw office — [Renishaw calibration service](https://www.renishaw.com/en/calibration-service-repair-and-recalibration--6877); [Renishaw calibration FAQs](https://www.renishaw.com/en/calibration-faqs--38657)
- Renishaw offers machine calibration and optimisation products (ballbar, laser, probing) and calibration training. The training listings found were UK/US only — [Renishaw machine calibration and optimisation](https://www.renishaw.com/en/machine-calibration-and-optimisation--6330); [Renishaw calibration training](https://www.renishaw.com/en/calibration-training--6810)

### Inferences
- A 30-machine shop could buy one QC20 ballbar kit and train its own maintenance staff, instead of relying on an outside service. Price not verified.
- On-machine probing setup (Renishaw OMP, Blum) is usually done through the machine OEM or the probe distributor.

### Gaps
- Not found or verified: Renishaw Vietnam office or distributor; Hexagon, Zeiss or API service providers in Vietnam; local ballbar or laser service pricing.

## 5. Korean public support programs (KITECH, KDMIC, KOTRA, MSS, KOICA, smart factory, retired-expert dispatch)

### Takeaway
**KITECH's Ho Chi Minh City office** is the most directly relevant Korean public resource. Its stated role is customized technical support for Korean firms operating locally, and KITECH also has a "해외 진출 기업 지원" (friendshoring) menu. KITECH's 디지털생산부문 (한국금형기술센터) is the domestic mold-tech arm. No currently open "retired-expert dispatch to overseas subsidiaries" program was found. KOICA's World Friends 자문단 targets partner-country development, not private Korean factories.

### Cited Findings
- KITECH (est. Oct 1989, government-funded) supports SMEs and mid-sized firms. Its core focus is root (뿌리) technologies, including casting, **mold**, heat treatment, surface treatment, plastic working and welding — [KITECH 임무 및 기능](https://www.kitech.re.kr/introduce/purpose); [Wikipedia: KITECH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korea_Institute_of_Industrial_Technology)
- KITECH lists an overseas office in **Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam**. The overseas office page gives customized technical support to Korean companies operating locally as a main duty — [KITECH 해외사무소](https://www.kitech.re.kr/corporate/global/abroad); [KITECH 조직도](https://www.kitech.re.kr/intro/organization)
- KITECH has a dedicated "해외 진출 기업 지원" (support for overseas-expanded firms / friendshoring) page. The page fetch failed, so its contents were not verified — [KITECH 해외 진출 기업 지원](https://www.kitech.re.kr/corporate/global/friendshoring)
- An interview (older; date uncertain) says the KITECH Vietnam office supports Korean firms locally and runs Korea–Vietnam government and industry technology-exchange programs — [Chào Việt Nam: KITECH 베트남 사무소장 인터뷰](http://www.chaovietnam.co.kr/archives/40320)
- KITECH held a "베트남 진출 한국 제조기업 간담회" at KOCHAM Hanoi on 17 Nov (year unclear in source) — [KITECH press release attachment](https://www.kitech.re.kr/api/v1/attachment/54a5e7ce-551d-4431-bd20-9315941b38f3/download)
- KITECH runs the Korea Mold Technology Center (한국금형기술센터, Bucheon, under 디지털생산부문) — [KITECH mold technology center brochure](https://mold.kitech.re.kr/bbs/pdf/mold%20technology%20center%20brochure.pdf); [Kyeonggi Ilbo 2016](https://www.kyeonggi.com/article/201604040845709)
- KITECH-linked mold-tech hub model abroad: a Korean-funded mold technology support hub opened in Cavite, Philippines, so this model exists in ASEAN — [PNA](https://alpha.pna.gov.ph/articles/1188295)
- KOICA World Friends 자문단 uses retired (or soon-to-retire) Korean experts with 10+ years of experience to build partner-country capacity, with "산업에너지" among its fields. Applications open twice a year. Its purpose is development cooperation, not support for private firms — [KOICA 자문단](https://koica.go.kr/koica_kr/948/subview.do)
- Old reporting puts KOICA retired-expert advisers at about US$4,500–4,750/month (dated) — [한국NGO신문](https://www.ngonews.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4536)
- KOICA–Hyundai Motor Group–Vietnam MOET MoU (2026): skilled-workforce training in die-casting, press forming and welding from H2 2026 to 2031. It is relevant to the local talent pool, not direct factory support — [Hyundai Motor Group news](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/en/news/CONT0000000000209898)
- KOICA has trained shipbuilding welders in Vietnam (53 graduates, 2025), an example of KOICA technical-training projects — [한국경제 2025-04-25](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202504256269i)
- Regional Korean agencies run 2026 Vietnam trade missions and export consultations (Busan BEPA, Chungbuk, Goryeong, Jeonbuk). These support market entry and sales, not process engineering — [BEPA 2026 베트남 무역사절단](https://trade.bepa.kr/support/delegation/info?seq=1491); [MTN: 전북경진원 베트남 지원](https://news.mtn.co.kr/article/2026101523174366867)

### Inferences
- Recommended first step: contact KITECH Vietnam (HCMC) and ask about (a) technical consulting or expert matching for 뿌리 (mold) firms abroad under the friendshoring program, and (b) links to 한국금형기술센터 experts for remote PowerMill/HSM process reviews.
- Korean domestic smart-factory subsidies (중기부/스마트제조혁신추진단) generally require a domestic Korean business. A Vietnam subsidiary is unlikely to qualify directly unless the Korean parent applies for domestic sites. This is not verified in this research.

### Gaps
- Not found or verified, including in current 2026 announcements: a KDMIC (한국금형공업협동조합) Vietnam support program; KOTRA HCMC technical-support services for manufacturers; MSS/KOSMES overseas-subsidiary technical support; a retired-technician overseas dispatch program by NIPA, KPC or KOSMES; KOSMES smart-factory eligibility for overseas sites.
- No Korean independent mold-machining or CAM consulting firms (other than YC and Delcam Korea) were found.

## 6. Taiwanese/Chinese/Vietnamese shoe-mold specialists and Vietnamese government programs

### Takeaway
Search did not surface any Taiwanese or Vietnamese shoe-mold consultancies or PowerMill service bureaus by name. There are local shoe-sole mold makers and CAM-capable shops in Binh Duong that could act as outsourced programming partners. ### Gaps
- No "VASI" source found. It is unclear whether the brief means the Vietnam Association for Supporting Industries (likely the HCMC-based HASI or the Hanoi-based VASI); not verified.
- No Taiwanese or Chinese shoe-mold consultants or CAM bureaus identified by name.

## 7. Independent global CNC/CAM consultants doing remote PowerMill optimization

### Takeaway
The documented remote options are Autodesk partners that sell PowerMill automation and post-processor work: OptiNC (plugin plus automation), E&C Solutions, Arkance and Trisita. No independent freelancers were verified. Udemy-style courses exist for self-training.

### Cited Findings
- OptiNC: PowerMill plugin, standardized CAM automation and custom post-processors — [Autodesk Partner Finder: OptiNC](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/optinc/937)
- E&C Solutions: bespoke posts, programming consultancy, process development, optimization and automation — [Autodesk Partner Finder: E&C Solutions](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/e--c-solutions/1893)
- Arkance: PowerMill macro automation and post tuning — [Autodesk success story](https://www.autodesk.com/support/partners/success-stories/innovation-und-przision-durch-die-partnerschaftliche-zusammenarbeit-zwischen-murtfeldt-und-auron/7932)
- Online PowerMill 4/5-axis courses exist on Udemy — [Udemy: Autodesk PowerMill 4axis 5axis training](https://www.udemy.com/course/autodesk-powermill-4axis-5axis-training/)

### Inferences
- A sensible engagement model: a fixed-scope remote project. It would include an audit of current PowerMill projects and NC output, a template and tool-database build, post verification for each machine control, and macro automation. It would be paired with an on-site week from a Korean partner (YC or Delcam Korea) or OneCAD.

### Gaps
- No pricing, day rates or Korean-language capability found for global consultants.
- Independent consultants outside the Autodesk Partner Finder could not be verified.
